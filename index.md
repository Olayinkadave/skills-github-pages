# MY CUP HALF FULL, OR HALF EMPTY
## The  Pessimists vs The Optimists
### Problems cannot be solved at the same level of thinking at which they were created". Where pessimists see problems (half empty), optimists find opportunities (half full). If you change the way you look at your problems, your problems change and transform into a rich array of opportunities to grow, learn and discover inner resources you never knew you had!
